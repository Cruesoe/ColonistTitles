[b]Shows each colonist's title under their name on the colonist bar and on the map.[/b]

Set a title in the Bio tab's rename dialog, and give each colonist their own title color so you can tell your doctors from your miners at a glance.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]The colonist's title appears under their name on the colonist bar and on the map. Colonists without a title can show their backstory title instead.
[*]Royal titles (Royalty) and ideoligion roles (Ideology) can appear as extra lines.
[*]Each colonist can have their own title color: click the palette button next to the rename button on their Bio tab.
[*]A full color picker with a color wheel, RGB, HSV and hex fields, and a palette.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Colonist bar and Map[/b]: choose which lines show in each place. By default the bar shows all three lines and the map shows none.
[*][b]Colors[/b]: the default title color, the royal title color, and the ideoligion role color (or each role's ideoligion color). A live preview shows the result.
[*][b]Display[/b]: label background and opacity.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with Colony Groups: titles show under names on its colonist bar.
Not compatible with Job In Bar or Titles For Me, which draw the same labels.
Safe to add to an existing save.
